El Salvadors most famous beach!

La Playa El Tunco

Playa El Tunco is a small, world-renowned beach town located along the Pacific coast in the La Libertad department of El Salvador. Centered within the country's celebrated "Surf City" region, this vibrant coastal destination is famous for its dramatic volcanic black sand, striking rock formations, and laid-back bohemian culture.
It consists primarily of two main streets packed with surf shops, local restaurants, and eco-hostels.

The iconic Pig Rock

The town takes its unique name from "El Tunco", a local Salvadoran slang word for pig. This refers to a massive, distinctive basalt rock formation jutting out of the water just off the shoreline.
To early inhabitants, the half-submerged boulder perfectly resembled the silhouette of a pig lying on its back, creating an enduring natural landmark that anchors the entire beach landscape today.
