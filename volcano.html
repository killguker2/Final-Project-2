El Salvadors most famous volcano !

The Volcano of Santa Ana & info :

The Santa Ana Volcano, also known natively as Ilamatepec ("hill of the old woman" in Nahuatl), is a massive, active stratovolcano and the highest volcanic peak in El Salvador at 2,381 meters (7,812 feet) above sea level.
Located in the western part of the country within the Los Volcanes National Park (Cerro Verde), it is famous for its stunning, highly acidic turquoise crater lake.

The Summit and Acid Crater Lake !

The defining feature of Santa Ana is its unique summit, which consists of four nested, superimposed craters that plunge roughly 300 meters deep.
At the absolute bottom of this main caldera sits a striking, highly acidic crater lake. This sulfuric lagoon continuously emanates volcanic gases and glows with a dramatic, shifting turquoise-green color,
though it is far too toxic for anyone to enter.
